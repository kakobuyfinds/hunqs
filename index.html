How to use the OrientDig spreadsheet

Match the item and size

Open the HipoBuyFind listing and choose OrientDig from the Buy Link menu where available. Match the color and size on the destination page. Compare the seller’s measurements in centimeters with a garment you already own.

Review warehouse QC photos

Check the size label, color, stitching and visible defects. For shoes, compare both sides and the soles. If a photo omits a detail that matters to you, check whether the agent can take an additional photo before shipping.

Estimate the delivered cost

Add the item price, domestic delivery, applicable service fees and international shipping. Check the carrier’s chargeable weight rules and allow for any duties or taxes at your destination.

OrientDig spreadsheet FAQ

Where do the product links go?

Each row opens the corresponding HipoBuyFind product page. Check its Buy Link menu for the available shopping platforms.

Does the displayed price include shipping?

The table shows the listed item price. Domestic and international shipping depend on the listing, parcel and destination.

What if the product link opens a different variant?

Compare the product photos, size chart and seller options before paying. If your chosen variant is missing, search HipoBuyFind by the item ID to find the original listing again.
